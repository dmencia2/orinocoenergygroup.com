What will you review before approving a purchase?
  • Supplier offer: specification, quantity, availability and quoted lead time.
  • Delivery scope: collection, packing, freight, customs and delivery items included in the quotation.
  • Decision points: technical alternatives, exclusions and information still needed before placing the order.
