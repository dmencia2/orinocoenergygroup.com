The group / the international connection

DISTINCT ROLES.
A SHARED PURPOSE.

Orinoco Energy Group brings procurement, energy services and logistics together. OEG Internacional handles international transport and distribution in the U.S. and Venezuela.

Houston procurement supports industrial supply. Our experience in the Orinoco and specialist companies support execution in Venezuela and the wider region.

OEG Internacional coordinates ocean, air and road transport, warehousing and delivery. OCK, ADUAVEN, ECO GREEN and TUBINCA provide port operations, customs, environmental services and technical inspection.

01 / Freight forwarding & NVOCC · U.S. & Venezuela

OEG Internacional

International freight, road transport, warehousing and distribution in the United States and Venezuela. OEG Internacional coordinates the journey from cargo pickup to final delivery.

  • Ocean and air freight, FCL/LCL and chartering coordination
  • Road transport, cargo pickup and final delivery in Venezuela and the U.S.
  • Warehousing, consolidation, staging and distribution
  • International project cargo and multimodal logistics
  • Supplier, procurement and shipment-readiness coordination

02 / Heavy lift · stevedoring · port operations

OCK Servicios Logísticos

More than 10 years of logistics and port experience, with over 440 vessels attended during that period. Operations include containers, bulk, general cargo, heavy machinery and simultaneous vessel handling.

  • Heavy-lift, breakbulk and industrial cargo handling
  • Stevedoring, loading, discharge and container operations
  • Vessel agency and representation

03 / Venezuela · customs

ADUAVEN

Venezuelan customs brokerage connecting import and export procedures with cargo documentation, port release and the onward logistics plan.

  • Venezuelan import and export customs clearance
  • Cargo classification and supporting trade documentation
  • Special customs regimes, transit and cargo-release coordination
ADUAVEN · equipment imports & Gaceta references

04 / Venezuela · environmental services

ECO GREEN

Industrial sanitation and environmental services supporting port, energy and industrial operations.

  • Industrial and well sanitation
  • Environmental remediation
  • Recovery and cleanup of operational areas

05 / Venezuela · asset integrity

TUBINCA

An allied energy-services company supporting industrial inspection, non-destructive testing and field infrastructure for oil exploration, production and refining.

  • Electromagnetic and ultrasonic testing
  • Hydrostatic and magnetic-particle inspection
  • Tubular integrity and API thread repair
  • Engineering, construction and field infrastructure
