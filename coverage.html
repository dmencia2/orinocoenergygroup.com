    The project defines the route

    THE RIGHT GATEWAY.
    SUPPLIER TO SITE.

    Container feeder networks are one part of industrial logistics. Equipment that needs breakbulk, heavy lift or chartering receives a cargo-specific transport and handling plan. Houston procurement connects with Gulf and South Florida gateways, including New Orleans, Tampa / Manatee, Miami and Port Everglades. Mexico, Colombia, Panama, Costa Rica and Brazil extend the regional connection. Houston-origin cargo also connects with Moín.

    Guanta connects with Maturín, Puerto Ordaz and the Orinoco. Maracaibo, Puerto Cabello, La Guaira and other Venezuelan gateways connect with the receiving industrial project and its inland delivery requirements. The network also serves project requirements through Venezuela’s other ports and inland destinations, with local handling coordinated at each location.

    International connections extend to China, Türkiye and the Middle East, with supplier and project development reaching Korea and Japan. The same coordination chain supports Venezuelan exports to the world.

    For breakbulk and industrial equipment, we compare Guanta, Maracaibo and other suitable gateways by cargo acceptance, lifting arrangements, terminal availability and the onward road route. The aim is an efficient journey to the receiving site, assessed across every stage.
