    THE RIGHT GATEWAY.
    SUPPLIER TO SITE.

    We coordinate procurement and cargo from Houston and other U.S. origins, Latin America, Europe and Asia. Your cargo, delivery destination and required date guide the route selection.

    Guanta is a key gateway for eastern Venezuela, including Maturín and the Orinoco. We also plan deliveries through Maracaibo, Puerto Cabello, La Guaira and other suitable ports across the country.

    Imports and exports follow the same approach: coordinate the supplier, international transport, customs and delivery. The port is one stage of the journey to the customer’s site.

    For industrial equipment, we compare suitable ports by handling capacity, vessel access, cargo acceptance and the inland journey. The decision considers total cost and delivery time from origin to site.
