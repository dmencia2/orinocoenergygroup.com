More than 20 years of experience in maritime and energy operations inform the group’s approach.

Orinoco Energy Group was built to connect the Orinoco’s energy and industrial potential with international supply, technical expertise and logistics. Houston procurement and our specialist network bring those capabilities into one project chain.

OEG Internacional provides international logistics, transport and distribution in the U.S. and Venezuela, while the group brings together port, customs, environmental and asset-integrity capabilities. Venezuela is our strongest connection, within a wider regional network.
